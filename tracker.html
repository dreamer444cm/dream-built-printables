Diet-Change Tracker

A calm place to review your pantry and log how your child is doing during a diet change, like a 30-day dye-free trial. This is an informal tool, not a diagnosis or medical advice. Talk with your child’s pediatrician before making big diet changes, and never stop prescribed medicine.

Privacy: Use a nickname only. No full names or diagnoses are needed. Everything is saved only on this device, in this browser. It is never sent anywhere, sold, or used for ads. Clearing your browser data or switching phones will erase it, so use Export to keep a copy. Accounts for saving across devices are coming.

Your entries save automatically. These buttons keep copies or make a printed report for a binder or appointment. Saving, printing, and exporting are always free.
